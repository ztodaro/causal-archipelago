PRIOR-ART: Kamdar et al. (2019), “Stars that Move Together Were Born Together,” The Astrophysical Journal Letters, 884, L42.
REASONING: This work directly anticipates the proposed mechanism: combine Gaia phase-space information with LAMOST metallicities, identify nearby comoving stellar pairs, and compare their metallicity differences with random-pair controls. Excess chemical similarity supports a common birth origin, including for pairs beyond ordinary bound-binary separations. The central inference—kinematic association independently corroborated by birth-cloud chemical homogeneity—and its survey-scale implementation are already present. Different separation cuts or more careful control matching could improve the analysis, but would not establish novelty for the mechanism described.
