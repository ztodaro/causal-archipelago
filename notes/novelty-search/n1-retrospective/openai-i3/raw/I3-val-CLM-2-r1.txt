PRIOR-ART: Krauss and Starkman, “Universal Limits on Computation” (2004), analyzing cosmological limits on sending out resource collectors and receiving their returns; standard conformal causal-diamond geometry.
REASONING: The core mechanism is the established round-trip causal-accessibility constraint in a universe with finite remaining conformal time. An outbound journey to comoving radius χ consumes conformal time χ/v, and a returning light signal consumes χ. Requiring their sum to fit within the remaining conformal-time budget immediately yields χ ≤ vχ_event/(1+v). The light-speed limit is the familiar half-event-horizon boundary for round-trip access. Replacing returned resources or energy with returned information does not change that mechanism. Extending the expression to arbitrary constant peculiar speed and emphasizing the reporting-volume fraction are useful presentations, but do not constitute a novel physical mechanism. Strictly, these horizon radii are suprema: signals from the boundary do not return at a finite time.
