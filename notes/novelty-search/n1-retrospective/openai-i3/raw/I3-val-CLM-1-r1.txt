PRIOR-ART: Simpson et al., “The GALAH survey: chemical tagging of comoving stars in Gaia DR1”; Andrews, Chanamé, and Agüeros’s work comparing spectroscopic metallicities of Gaia/TGAS wide binaries with unrelated stellar pairs.
REASONING: Published work already tests whether kinematically selected comoving stars share a birth origin by measuring chemical similarity. The GALAH work directly examines chemical consistency among Gaia comoving candidates, while wide-binary studies cross-match astrometric pair catalogs with spectroscopic surveys and compare within-pair metallicity differences against unrelated pairs. These anticipate the central causal premise, independent chemical validation channel, survey cross-matching, and pair-difference comparison. Restricting the interpretation to dissolved-cluster siblings or improving control matching could produce a useful new analysis, but does not establish a novel core mechanism. Chemical agreement also supports rather than uniquely certifies common origin because unrelated stars can have similar abundances.
