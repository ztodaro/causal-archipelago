REASONING: For records originating at events (x_i,t_i), earliest common receipt minimizes max_i[t_i + ||x−x_i||/c] over receipt location x. This is an additively weighted Euclidean minimax location problem, expressible as a second-order cone program. Simultaneous origins give precisely the minimum enclosing ball problem. Pairwise sufficiency fails for familiar geometric reasons: an equilateral triangle or regular tetrahedron requires a larger enclosing radius than half its diameter. Triple and tetrahedral binding conditions are standard support-set geometry, consistent with Helly’s theorem. Content weights introduce subset selection and deadline-dependent value frontiers but do not establish a new underlying mechanism.
