PRIOR-ART: Brunn’s concavity theorem for volumes of parallel sections of convex bodies, and the classical Brunn–Minkowski argument for Minkowski symmetrization.
REASONING: The stated optimization mechanism reproduces a classical inequality: if [D(z) + (−D(z))]/2 ⊆ D(0), Brunn–Minkowski gives |D(0)| ≥ |[D(z) + (−D(z))]/2| ≥ |D(z)|. In the equivalent jointly convex, centrally symmetric family formulation, this is the theorem that the central section has maximal volume. Consequently, the symmetrization principle itself is established prior art. Potentially original contributions are showing that the expanding-universe reporting model satisfies the required inclusion, covering arbitrary probe speeds and processing delays, and deriving the two-collector union formula. I do not know a published treatment anticipating those precise applications. This verdict concerns the expressly identified core mathematical mechanism, not a claim that the complete cosmological result has already appeared.
