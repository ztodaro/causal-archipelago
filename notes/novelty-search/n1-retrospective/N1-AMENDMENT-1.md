# N1 Amendment 1 — prospective-arm procedure refinements (FROZEN)

Date: 2026-08-31. Dated amendment per the E12c pattern; the base spec
(N1-SELF-AUDIT-CALIBRATION-SPEC.md, e972ae7) is not edited. Authority: the project owner, 2026-08-31; the owner may overturn any clause before the prospective arm's first claim is
instrumented, after which changes require a further dated amendment.

## A1.1 Verdict tiers replace the binary

Judges answer in four tiers — ANTICIPATED / SUBSTRATE-IMPLIED (obvious
composition of located work) / AS-APPLICATION (mechanism anticipated,
composite unlocated) / UNLOCATED — with confidence per tier. Evidence:
both labs' unanimous-opposite CLM-4 split and the CLM-6 outcome were
artifacts of forcing penumbra claims through NOVEL/NOT-NOVEL; the
open-book R cell landed naturally on the tier reading. Primary
endpoints move to tier agreement with G and locate-rate; binary
accuracy is retired.

## A1.2 G labels settled before collection, with scope stated

Every prospective claim's ground truth is labeled at the MECHANISM
level and the CONFIGURATION level separately, before any judgment is
collected, with the anticipating sources named per level. Evidence:
CLM-4 and CLM-6 both failed as binary labels for scope reasons the
two levels would have separated.

## A1.3 Recency sweep is mandatory in G searches

A kill-budget G search must include a sweep of the last 60 days of
relevant arXiv listings (not only keyword search), and every G label
carries its search date and is re-verified if more than 14 days old
when judging begins. Evidence: the CLM-6 label missed Boufourou
(arXiv:2608.24556) at five days' distance — a measured decay constant
on a certified frontier point of about a week, in an active dispute.

## A1.4 Locate criteria (Rulings 1–3 as standing precedent)

Strict = names the G-listed source or an exact mathematical synonym
of its frame (flag complex = Vietoris–Rips). Material = describes
mechanism-anticipating published work, CREDITED ONLY AFTER
source-level verification that the named work carries the mechanism —
the Argyris downgrade is the precedent; unverified family
attributions score 0 until checked. One harmonized rule set across
all lanes and labs, frozen with the tranche's G labels.

## A1.5 R is mandatory, and union coverage is a primary endpoint

Every prospective claim gets at least one isolated retrieval-grounded
lane. Retrieval endpoints are scored per lane AND as union coverage
across lanes, because the retrospective record shows lanes' strict
misses are decorrelated across labs (Čech–Rips vs Bloor) and even
across query vocabularies within one open-book lane (Olson 2022 found
by R(CLM-4)'s queries, missed by R(CLM-2)'s). The Olson case is the
existence proof that closed-book union coverage has a ceiling
strictly below 100%.

## A1.6 Dual-reporting rule for any post-hoc G revision

A G label found wrong after judging (CLM-6 → ANTICIPATED, Boufourou
2026) is revised only by dated entry, with all affected scoreboards
reported under both the original and revised labels, never silently
rescored. This amendment records the first such revision.
