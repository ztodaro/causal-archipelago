# E1c, correction (19 October 2026)

`E1C-SPEC.md` and `E1C-RESULTS.md` stay as stamped. An independent review on 4 September 2026 measured that the orbit-matched controls were looser than E1c reported. A rerun of that review's checks on 19 October reproduced its numbers exactly.

## The controls were less orbit-coherent than the treatment pairs

Each control star had to sit within the tolerance of the treatment pair's mean actions. Two control stars could therefore differ from each other by twice the tolerance. In GALAH the control pairs ended up less alike in orbit than the treatment pairs (absolute within-pair action differences, kpc km/s):

| Action | Treatment median | Control median | Control pairs over the tolerance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lz | 6.71 | 9.90 | 25% |
| Jz | 0.116 | 0.218 | 24% |
| Jr | 0.535 | 1.030 | 24% |

How much of the remaining chemistry contrast this imbalance explains has not been measured.

## What stands

The measured C4 contrasts stand: −0.055 dex in GALAH and −0.033 dex in APOGEE. The sentence in `E1C-RESULTS.md` that calls the remainder "birth memory at high significance in both surveys" goes further than these controls support. The supported reading is a residual similarity in iron abundance under approximate orbit controls. The share removed by orbit matching ("roughly half") is provisional.

A firmer test would match the pairs' within-pair action differences as well as their mean actions, along with age information and measurement conditions.
